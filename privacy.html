OBS-BOT Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

OBS-BOT is a tool for managing and publishing original gaming videos to accounts authorized by their owners.

OBS-BOT may process account identifiers, authorization tokens and video files necessary to provide publishing functionality.

Authentication credentials are stored on the application's server and are not sold to third parties.

Users can revoke TikTok authorization through their TikTok account settings.

Data is retained only as necessary to operate the publishing service and maintain records.
